Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025  |  Effective immediately

We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

1. Who we are

CHEAH ZI KANG (Registration No: 202603101298 (003842610-M)) is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are registered and operate in Malaysia.

Contact for privacy matters: diair.my@gmail.com

2. What data we collect

When you use our water vending service, we may collect the following data:

  • Transaction data: Payment amount, order ID, transaction timestamp, device location
  • Payment data: Payment method used (e.g. DuitNow, Touch 'n Go). We do NOT store card numbers, bank account numbers, or eWallet credentials — these are handled entirely by Fiuu
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type (collected automatically when you visit our payment page)

We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number unless you voluntarily contact us for support.

3. How we use your data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

  • Processing your payment and dispensing water
  • Detecting and preventing fraud or unauthorised use
  • Resolving disputes and processing refunds
  • Maintaining and improving our service
  • Complying with legal obligations

4. Data sharing

We share data only with the following parties and only as necessary:

  • Fiuu Sdn Bhd (formerly Razer Merchant Services): Our payment gateway, licensed by Bank Negara Malaysia. They process payments on our behalf and have their own privacy policy.
  • Happy-ti (HPT): Our machine management platform, which receives payment signals to control the water dispensing machine.
  • Netlify Inc: Our website hosting provider, which may collect standard server logs.
  • Law enforcement or regulators: If required by law or court order.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Data retention

Transaction records are retained for 7 years in accordance with Malaysian financial record-keeping requirements. Technical logs are retained for up to 90 days. Support correspondence is retained for 2 years.

6. Cookies

Our payment page does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We may use essential session data to maintain your payment session, which expires automatically after your transaction is complete.

7. Your rights under PDPA

Under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Withdraw consent for processing (where processing is based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Department of Personal Data Protection Malaysia

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at diair.my@gmail.com. We will respond within 21 days.

8. Data security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted communications (HTTPS), access controls, and regular security reviews. Payment data is handled by Fiuu using PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure.

9. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

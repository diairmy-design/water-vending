About us
Bringing clean water closer to communities

CHEAH ZI KANG operates community water vending machines across Malaysia, providing affordable access to purified drinking water. We believe everyone deserves clean water at a fair price.

Our machines use multi-stage filtration technology and are regularly serviced and tested. All payments are handled through a licensed and regulated payment gateway — no cash, no hassle.

We are registered under the Companies Commission of Malaysia (SSM) and operate in full compliance with local regulations.

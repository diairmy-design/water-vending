Refund Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025  |  We make refunds simple and automatic

Full refund if no water dispensed
Automatic — no need to request

Our refund commitment

We stand behind the reliability of our service. If you pay for water and the machine fails to dispense it, you will receive a full refund automatically — no need to contact us or submit a form.

Our system monitors every transaction. If the machine does not confirm successful dispensing within 10 minutes of payment, our automated system initiates the refund immediately through Fiuu (our payment gateway).

When you are entitled to a refund

  • Machine does not dispense water after successful payment — full refund, automatic
  • Machine dispensed less water than paid for due to a technical fault — partial or full refund upon request
  • Double charge for a single transaction — duplicate charge refunded automatically
  • Payment deducted but transaction failed before dispensing — full refund, automatic

When refunds do not apply

  • You changed your mind after payment and water was successfully dispensed
  • You did not collect the water after it was dispensed
  • The machine dispensed the correct amount but you believe it was insufficient (water volume is calibrated to the amount paid)
  • Disputes about water quality after the water has been collected and consumed

How automatic refunds work

1
Payment confirmed

You pay via your chosen method. Fiuu confirms the payment to our system.

2
Dispensing signal sent

Our system sends the signal to the machine within seconds. The machine begins dispensing.

3
Confirmation monitored

Our system checks every 30 seconds for 10 minutes to confirm the machine received and processed the signal.

4
Automatic refund if no confirmation

If the machine does not confirm within 10 minutes (e.g. machine offline, hardware fault), our system automatically initiates a full refund through Fiuu. No action required from you.

Refund timeline

Once a refund is initiated by our system, the time for funds to appear in your account depends on your payment method:

  • DuitNow QR / FPX (bank transfer): 3–7 business days
  • Touch 'n Go eWallet: 1–5 business days
  • Alipay: 3–7 business days
  • Visa / Mastercard: 5–14 business days (depends on your card issuer)

These timelines are governed by Fiuu and the respective banks/eWallet providers, not by us. We initiate the refund immediately but cannot control processing time at the bank level.

Requesting a manual refund

If you believe you are entitled to a refund for a situation not covered by our automatic system, please contact us within 7 days of the transaction with:

  • Your transaction date and time
  • The amount paid
  • The payment method used
  • A brief description of the issue
  • Your transaction reference number (shown in your eWallet / bank statement)

We will investigate and respond within 3 business days.

Chargebacks

We encourage you to contact us directly before initiating a chargeback with your bank or card provider. We respond quickly to all refund requests and prefer to resolve issues directly. Unjustified chargebacks may affect your ability to use our service in future.

Need help with a refund?

Our team responds within 1 business day. Have your transaction reference ready.
